# Films

Two cuts of the same 1:35 short, both scored to the same track and cut on its
beat (135 bpm, one scene every three bars).

| file | what it is | size |
|---|---|---|
| `laiba-illustrated-1440p.mp4` | hand-painted version — every photograph redrawn as an illustration, her likeness kept | 1440×2560 |
| `laiba-real-1080p.mp4` | the same film with the original photographs | 1080×1920 |
| `laiba-mixed-1080p.mp4` | the two alternating — photograph, painting, photograph | 1080×1920 |
| `laiba-shortfilm-1080p.mp4` | **the short film** — a story in four acts, not a slideshow | 1080×1920 |
| `laiba-directors-cut-1080p.mp4` | **the director's cut** — title card, act cards, rain and stars, a beat-cut montage, the payoff shots | 1080×1920 |

All three open on the locket, which swings open on its chain to show her face,, carry a couplet from Momin and one from Faraz, run
eleven portraits each with its own name, and close on `Rafay ki Zindagi`.

## The short film

`laiba-shortfilm-1080p.mp4` is the one with a story. A boy on a rooftop at
night writes the sentence three times and deletes it three times; she is
awake at her own window; a split screen holds them apart; his memories of
her carry her names; two hands nearly touch; the words finally get sent;
dawn comes up over the city; and the locket that opened in the first shot
is answered by the two of them in the last.

Cut on the beat throughout, letterboxed until the final act.
