Chapter I · Aamad

Tumhara aana aur bina kahe sab badal gaya.

Koi dramatic shuruaat nahi thi. Koi perfect timing nahi. Bas tumhari aamad hui — apni garmahat, apni sacchai, aur aam si cheezon ko zinda kar dene ke apne andaaz ke saath.

Chapter III · Yaadein

Maine woh lamhe rakhe jo tumhe yaad bhi na hon.

Isliye nahi ki woh bade the, balki isliye ki woh humare the—chahe sirf kuch second ke liye.

“Sabse chhote lamhe bhi khaas ban gaye, sirf isliye ki unmein tum the.”

Chapter IV · Choti Baatein

Pyaar un cheezon se hua jo tumne dikhayi hi nahi.

01 · Narmi

Jab koi nahi dekh raha hota, tab bhi tumhara khayal rakhna.

Yeh sahaj hai, khamosh hai, aur kabhi dikhawe ke liye nahi.

02 · Sukoon

Tumhare saath khamoshi bhi aasan lagti hai.

Har second ko bharne ki zaroorat nahi, jab tumhara hona hi kaafi hai.

03 · Roshni

Tumhari muskurahat poore kamre ko badal deti hai.

Woh logon tak pahunch jaati hai, isse pehle ki tumhe pata chale ki tumne kya diya.

04 · Sacchai

Tumhare saath sab kuch sacha lagta hai.

Na koi game, na koi dikhawa—bas kuch sacha aur bahut kam milne wala.

Yaqeen nahi. Imkaan.

Chapter V · Imkaan

Nahi chahiye perfect future.

Mujhe bas ek mauka chahiye yeh jaanne ka ki kya ho sakta hai — agar hum ek dusre ko chunein, sabr ke saath, sacchai ke saath, aur shuru karne ki himmat ke saath.
